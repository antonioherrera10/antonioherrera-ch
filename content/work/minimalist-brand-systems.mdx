## The Concept

We developed a comprehensive sensory identity system based on extreme clarity, high contrast typography, and premium Swiss grids.

## The Strategy

Establishing a luxury position by saying more with less. Standard templates were eliminated to focus purely on high-end raw craftsmanship.
