## The Vision

How do you translate the physical weight of art into pixels? This digital exhibition space replicates high-end spatial geometry through interactive layouts, raw web typography, and custom loaders.

## The Platform

Engineered as a full-stack, fully static Next.js application with bespoke gesture navigation.
