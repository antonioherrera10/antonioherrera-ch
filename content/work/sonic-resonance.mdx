## The Vision

Connecting people through shared physical resonance and uninhibited expression. Sonic Resonance was developed as an immersive performance to bridge classical vocal techniques and experimental electronic soundscapes.

## The Design

We utilized raw industrial concrete halls as natural reverb chambers, overlaying synchronized lighting stripes in our signature brand-red, brand-orange, and brand-rosa colors.
