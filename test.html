Cool neutrals beside warm colour is the oldest move in luxury for making each hue read crisply, and it removes any brown cast entirely. The graphite base is cooler than a warm black, so Identity, Work and Spaces separate further. Platinum stays the connective accent.

04

Where I land

Option 03 on a cool spine is my pick. It solves the brown most decisively, the warm hues read crisply against cool graphite, and platinum ties it together without gold. Option 01 is the cleanest if you want one quiet accent across everything. Option 02 is the reference for the full eight-colour system and the methods.
